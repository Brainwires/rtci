# RFC 9143: Negotiating Media Multiplexing Using SDP (BUNDLE): clause audit

Spec: RFC 9143, February 2022. It obsoletes RFC 8843 (the number the code's comments cite) and updates
RFC 3264, 5888 and 7941. What changed from RFC 8843 that matters here: section 7.1.3 / 7.3 / 7.5 now say
BUNDLE (IDENTICAL and TRANSPORT) attributes go only in the tagged m= section in every answer and in
every subsequent offer (RFC 8843 required the offerer of a subsequent offer to repeat them, with
identical values, in each bundled section), and the 3PCC text of 7.6 was added. At the audit the code
followed neither text: it repeated the BUNDLE attributes in every bundled m= section of every offer
and answer. It now follows RFC 9143 by default (row 7.1.3-3); the repeated form is an opt-in.
Audited at fork master 8916bc3.

At the audit the engine had one transport (docs/conformance.md, decision D2). Since mt-2 to mt-8
each BUNDLE group, and each section outside one, is a transport of its own (`transport_layout.rs`):
an initial `balanced` / `max-compat` offer gives sections unique addresses, an answer without a
group or with several is followed, a later remote offer may move a section into a group or out of
it. This side's offers move a section out of a group, or back, when the application asks
(`RTCRtpTransceiver::set_bundle_request`, mt-9; 7.5.2-1).

Path legend: `pc/` = `src/peer_connection/`, `sdp/mod.rs` = `src/peer_connection/sdp/mod.rs`,
`me.rs` = `src/peer_connection/configuration/media_engine.rs`, `icpt` = `src/peer_connection/handler/interceptor.rs`,
`endp` = `src/peer_connection/handler/endpoint.rs`.

## Gaps, in order of importance

1. **Read BUNDLE groups by semantics; several groups; validate the answer's group (S).** RFC9143-7.3-1,
   5-1, 5-2, 7.4-1, 6-1, 7.3.1-2.
2. **Payload-type association and MID re-association on receive (M).** RFC9143-9.2-9, 9.2-5, 9.2-2,
   9.2-10: build the payload-type table, fall back to it for an undeclared SSRC without MID, move an SSRC
   when its MID changes, keep going when a MID is unknown.
3. **RTCP SDES MID (M).** RFC9143-9.2-12, 15-1, 9.2-1: add SDES item 15 to `rtci-rtcp`, send it with
   CNAME, apply received ones.
4. **MID header extension on send (M).** RFC9143-15-2, 9.1-3: always offer `sdes:mid` when a BUNDLE
   group is offered, and stamp it (first packets and on SSRC change) in the sender.
5. **BUNDLE attribute placement (M).** RFC9143-7.1.3-3, 7.5-1, 10-1, 9.3.1.2-1: ICE, DTLS, rtcp-mux
   attributes only in the tagged section in answers and subsequent offers. Interop risk is low
   (browsers accept the repeated form) but it is what the current text requires.
6. **True initial offers: bundle-only or per-section transports (M to XL).** RFC9143-7.2-1, 7.2-4, 6-2,
   7.3.2-1, 7.5.2-1, 7.4-3 (see rfc9429.md gap 9).
7. **rtcp-mux-only and reading rtcp-mux (S).** RFC9143-9.3-2, 9.3.1.2-2, 9.3.1.1-2, 9.3.1.3-1 (rfc8858.md).
8. **Validation of remote descriptions (S).** RFC9143-9.1-2, 9.1.1-1, 12-1, 8-1, 9.1-4.
9. **Tests only.** The `untested` rows.
