# RFC 4588: RTP Retransmission Payload Format (RTX)

Spec: RFC 4588, July 2006. Audited against fork master 8916bc3. Scope: sections 3.1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8
(payload-format parameters and their SDP mapping), 12. Section 9 (RTSP) and 10.2 (layered multicast)
are `n/a`: the engine has no RTSP and no multicast. Only SSRC-multiplexing exists (one BUNDLE transport,
`a=ssrc-group:FID`); every session-multiplexing clause is `missing` or `n/a` as marked.

Where it lives:

- Sender: `NackResponderInterceptor` (`rtci-interceptor/src/nack/responder.rs`) buffers sent packets per
  SSRC and, on a generic NACK, emits an RTX packet (when the stream was bound with `ssrc_rtx` and
  `payload_type_rtx`) or the original packet again (when RTX was not negotiated).
- Receiver: `InterceptorHandler::deencapsulate_if_rtx` (`src/peer_connection/handler/interceptor.rs:290`)
  rewrites an RTX packet into the original before the interceptor chain; association is by
  `a=ssrc-group:FID` (`resolve_rtx_primary`, `handler/endpoint.rs:456`) or by `repaired-rtp-stream-id`
  (`bind_repair_by_rrid`, `handler/interceptor.rs:592`).
- Requests: `NackGeneratorInterceptor` (`rtci-interceptor/src/nack/generator.rs`).
- SDP: `video/rtx` with `apt=` in the default codecs (`media_engine.rs`), `find_rtx_payload_type` /
  `parse_rtx_apt` (`src/rtp_transceiver/rtp_sender/rtp_codec.rs:267`, `:297`).

## Gaps, in order of importance

1. **NACK generator covers repair streams and ignores BYE (S).** IDs 6.3-7, 5.3-4. Bind the RTX and
   FEC SSRCs with an empty feedback list (or teach the generator to skip `StreamInfo`s that are a
   repair flow), and unbind a remote stream (or at least clear its receive log) when its BYE arrives.
2. **RTCP for the RTX stream (M).** IDs 6.1-1, 6.2-1, 5.2-1. Send SR + SDES CNAME for the RTX SSRC
   (count RTX packets and octets in `SenderReportInterceptor`), and feed the receiver-report stream of
   the RTX SSRC from the RTX packets as they arrived, before de-encapsulation. Decide at the same time
   what the primary's reception report should count (see Bugs 3).
3. **Request policy (M).** IDs 6.3-2, 6.3-3, 6.3-4, 6.3-5, 8.1-3, A-1. RTT-based re-request timer
   (RTT is already measured for stats), a default reorder allowance, a default cap on requests per
   packet, a usefulness deadline (jitter-buffer target or `rtx-time`), and honouring the peer's
   `rtx-time`.
4. **Random initial RTX sequence number, kept across re-binds (S).** ID 4-7.
5. **SSRC collision handling for original/RTX pairs (M).** IDs 4-3, 4-4. Needs the RFC 3550 collision
   machinery first; at minimum reject `rtx.ssrc == ssrc` when SSRCs are supplied.
6. **RTX clock-rate check (S).** ID 4-10: reject or ignore an `rtx` codec whose clock rate differs
   from its `apt` codec's, locally registered or remote.
7. **Unsignalled association (M).** IDs 5.3-1, 5.3-5: associate by outstanding NACK when an RTX SSRC
   is neither in a FID group nor carries a repaired-RID; re-associate a declared stream after an SSRC
   change.
8. **Session-multiplexing (L, likely never).** ID 3.1-1. Not used by WebRTC; list it under "Not
   implemented, on purpose".
9. **Tests for what is implemented but unpinned (S).** IDs 4-6, 4-18, 12-1, 12-2.

## Bugs found

1. **The receiver NACKs the RTX and FEC streams.** `handler/interceptor.rs:425-452` (and `:829-856` for RID streams) binds the RTX SSRC
   and the FEC SSRC as remote streams with the primary codec (`&codec`), whose `rtcp_feedback` contains
   `nack`; `NackGeneratorInterceptor::bind_remote_stream` (`nack/generator.rs:262`) therefore opens a
   receive log for each. RTX packets with a payload are rewritten to the primary SSRC before the chain,
   but padding-only RTX packets are not (`endpoint.rs:504` returns false, by design), so the RTX log
   sees only the probes and treats every sequence number between two probes as lost. Reproduce (unit,
   no network): build a chain with `NackGeneratorBuilder`, bind the primary `StreamInfo` and a second
   `StreamInfo { ssrc: RTX_SSRC, rtcp_feedback: [nack] }` exactly as `bind_declared_ssrc` does, feed
   RTX-SSRC packets with empty payload at sequence numbers 10 and 20, advance 100 ms, and read a
   `TransportLayerNack { media_ssrc: RTX_SSRC, .. }` for 11..19; advance again and it repeats.
   End-to-end: through `InterceptorHandler::handle_read` with a declared FID pair, same input.
2. **NACKs continue after BYE, and for any gap left open at the end of a stream.**
   `nack/generator.rs:147-196`: `missing_seq_numbers` is recomputed on every tick and a NACK is emitted
   whenever it is non-empty; with the default `max_nacks_per_packet = 0` there is no cap, and nothing
   removes the log except `unbind_remote_stream`, which `process_goodbyes`
   (`handler/interceptor.rs:195`) does not call. Reproduce: feed sequence numbers 1, 2, 4, then a BYE
   for the SSRC through `InterceptorHandler::handle_read`; drive `handle_timeout` for 10 s; count
   NACKs for sequence 3 (expected 0 after the BYE, observed about 100).
3. **A retransmitted packet is counted as an original arrival in the primary stream's reception
   report.** `handler/interceptor.rs:1011` rewrites RTX into the primary SSRC and sequence number
   before `ReceiverReportInterceptor` sees it, on purpose (comment at `:1001`). The effect on the wire:
   `fraction lost` / `cumulative lost` for the original SSRC report post-repair loss, so a remote
   loss-based congestion controller that reads RRs sees no loss on a lossy path, while the RTX SSRC's
   own report shows nothing received. RFC 4588 3 chose a separate stream precisely so RTCP statistics
   stay correct per stream. Reproduce: through `InterceptorHandler`, deliver primary 1, 2, 4 and an RTX
   packet with OSN 3; take the next RR for the primary SSRC and assert `total_lost == 1` (observed 0);
   take the RR for the RTX SSRC and assert its extended highest sequence number advanced (it has not).
4. **RTX sequence numbers restart at 0 on every re-bind.** `nack/responder.rs:313-330` builds a fresh
   `LocalStream { rtx_sequence_number: 0, .. }` in `bind_local_stream`, and a renegotiation that changes
   the codec re-binds with the same SSRCs (`rebind_local_streams`). The RTX SSRC then reuses sequence
   numbers; with SRTP this replays packet indices (same SSRC, same ROC, same sequence number), which
   `SrtpHandler::handle_write` drops as `SrtpIndexReused` (`handler/srtp.rs:155`; `rtci-srtp/src/context/srtp.rs:133` refuses an index that already protected a different packet), so retransmissions
   silently stop working until the counter passes its old high-water mark. Reproduce: bind, retransmit
   5 packets over RTX, unbind and bind the same `StreamInfo`, retransmit again; the RTX sequence
   numbers are 0..4 twice. Then run the same through `SrtpHandler` and see the second batch dropped.
5. **Retransmission without RTX can be dropped by the SRTP layer (not verified end to end).**
   `responder.rs:232` resends the original packet on the original SSRC and sequence number.
   `rtci-srtp/src/context/srtp.rs:128-133` protects the same index again only when the plaintext is
   byte-identical, and refuses it when it "lies more than a window behind the highest index sent".
   Any interceptor nearer the wire that rewrites the packet (the TWCC sender stamps a new
   transport-wide sequence number on every packet it sees, retransmissions included) changes the bytes,
   so the retransmission is refused and `SrtpHandler::handle_write` (`handler/srtp.rs:155`) drops it
   silently. Check with a chain `NackResponder + TwccSender -> SrtpHandler`, a stream bound without
   `ssrc_rtx`: NACK a sent packet and look for a datagram.

## Since the audit

- **Bug 1 fixed.** Repair flows are bound without `nack` / `ccm` feedback (`repair_flow_codec`). Test:
  `gaps_between_rtx_padding_probes_are_not_nacked`.
- **Bug 2 fixed** for the BYE: the NACK generator drops a source's receive log on its BYE. Test:
  `a_gap_is_not_requested_after_the_sources_bye`. (A gap left open by a stream that simply stops is
  the request policy's, gap 3.)
- **Bug 4 fixed.** The responder keeps the next sequence number of each RTX SSRC over a re-bind and
  starts a new RTX stream at a random one. Tests:
  `rtx_sequence_numbers_carry_on_when_the_stream_is_bound_again`,
  `rtx_sequence_numbers_do_not_start_at_a_fixed_value`.
- **Bug 3 fixed.** A retransmission keeps what it was on the wire (`Attribute::Retransmitted`) and
  the RTCP session counts it on the RTX stream. Test:
  `a_retransmission_is_reported_on_the_rtx_stream`.
- **Gaps 2 and 3 closed**: SR and CNAME for the RTX SSRC; the request policy (ten requests, two
  seconds, a round trip between requests, an optional reordering delay). gcc-1 (after
  v0.22.0+brainwires.6): the ten requests and two seconds are settings now, not the default, and
  retransmissions are held to the congestion controller's target (RFC4588-6.3-2, -7-1, -A-1). `rtx-time` has both ends'
  options and no SDP wiring (8.1-3).
