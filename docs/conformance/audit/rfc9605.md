# RFC 9605 (Secure Frame, SFrame): clause-level audit

Spec: RFC 9605, "Secure Frame (SFrame): Lightweight Authenticated Encryption for Real-Time Media", August 2024 (https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9605.txt). Engine: fork master 8916bc3, plus the `rtc-sframe` crate.

Finding (at 8916bc3): the repository had no SFrame implementation. The `rtc-sframe` crate now implements sections 4 and 5, the sender's and the receiver's parts of sections 7 and 9, and is checked against every test vector of Appendix C. It is a standalone crate: nothing in the engine calls it, and the application applies it to a frame before packetizing or to a packet's payload. What is still `missing` is the RTP side (sections 6.1.3 and 6.2, and the `RFC9605-X-*` rows): a format-agnostic packetizer for encrypted frames, SDP signalling, and forwarding metadata. Clauses addressed to the key management system or the application's KID strategy stay `n/a`. The rows `RFC9605-X-*` at the end are not RFC clauses: they say what the engine exposes for an application that uses SFrame.

All RFC 2119 sentences of the RFC are listed (19), plus the unkeyworded format and algorithm definitions of section 4 as MUST-level features, since an implementation that does not follow them does not interoperate.

## Gaps, in order of importance

1. **An SFrame implementation (L).** Done: `rtc-sframe`.
2. **RTP packetization of SFrame (M).** `RFC9605-6.1-1`, `RFC9605-6.2-1`, `RFC9605-X-3`, `RFC9605-X-5`: the SFrame RTP payload format (per-frame with generic fragmentation, per-packet), its SDP signalling and a transceiver switch (shared with `ENCTRANS-2.1-1`).
3. **Layers and forwarding metadata (M).** `RFC9605-6.1.3-1`, `RFC9605-X-6`: one ciphertext per layer and the dependency descriptor (shared with the SVC package).
4. **A test for opaque payloads (S).** `RFC9605-X-4`.
