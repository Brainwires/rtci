Connection ids are implemented for DTLS 1.3 (RFC 9147 section 9, `rfc9147.md`), which takes the `connection_id` extension and the address rule of section 6 from this RFC. The DTLS 1.2 part, the `tls12_cid` record with its changed MAC and additional data, is not implemented: a hello that offers DTLS 1.2 does not carry the extension.

## Gaps, in order of importance

1. **Connection ID for DTLS 1.2 (L).** Every `missing` row is one work package: the extension, the
   tls12_cid record format with inner plaintext and padding, the three MAC / additional-data
   constructions, demultiplexing by CID in `Endpoint` (today keyed by remote address), and the
   address-update rule. WebRTC does not use it: ICE consent and the selected pair already carry an
   association across address changes, and browsers do not offer the extension. It stays in "Not
   implemented, on purpose" unless the owner wants the literal 100%.
2. **Test that an offered connection_id is ignored (S).** RFC9146-3-8.

## Gaps, in order of importance

1. **The tls12_cid record for DTLS 1.2 (L).** RFC9146-3-4 to -3-7, -4-1, -5-1, -5-2. The work: negotiate the extension in the DTLS 1.2 hellos (`flight0.rs`, `flight3.rs`, `flight4.rs`); give `CipherSuite` a second pair of entry points that take the connection id, and in each of the four record ciphers (`crypto_gcm.rs`, `crypto_ccm.rs`, `crypto_chacha20.rs`, `crypto_cbc.rs`) build the additional data, or the MAC input, of section 5 (`seq_num_placeholder`, `tls12_cid`, the cid and its length) over a DTLSInnerPlaintext; teach `split_datagram` and `handle_incoming_packet` the longer header of a type-25 record; and let `Endpoint::migrate` find a DTLS 1.2 association by its id. Then the extension can be sent by every hello, which closes RFC9147-5.1-14. WebRTC has no use for it: ICE moves an association between addresses. Whether OpenSSL can be the other end for known answers has to be checked first (its `s_server` has no connection id switch in 4.1.0).
2. **A test that a DTLS 1.2 server ignores the offer (S).** RFC9146-3-10.
