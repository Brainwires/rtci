## Gaps, in order of importance

1. **Connection ID for DTLS 1.2 (L).** Every `missing` row is one work package: the extension, the
   tls12_cid record format with inner plaintext and padding, the three MAC / additional-data
   constructions, demultiplexing by CID in `Endpoint` (today keyed by remote address), and the
   address-update rule. WebRTC does not use it: ICE consent and the selected pair already carry an
   association across address changes, and browsers do not offer the extension. It stays in "Not
   implemented, on purpose" unless the owner wants the literal 100%.
2. **Test that an offered connection_id is ignored (S).** RFC9146-3-8.
